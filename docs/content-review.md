# Content review: jokes vs. tone rules

**Rules checked** (from the brief):
1. Jokes target the behaviour, the situation, the myths or the apps, never the player and never the harm.
2. No victim-blaming ("you should have…"). The narrator is always on the player's side.
3. Nothing graphic; scenes stop before harm.
4. The "polite do-nothing" option may lead to the funny-bad ending, but the text never says it was her fault.

**Target key:** B = behaviour of the harasser · S = situation · M = myth/social norm · A = app/institution · P = the player (not allowed)

Reviewed 10 Oct 2026. Every ending page also carries the same disclaimer: *"…nothing that happens to you is your fault. These are ideas, not rules."* An automated test fails if any ending page contains "you should".

## Items to watch in user testing
- Commute 2C was rewritten ("the national reflex: saying sorry for being touched"). Check participants read it as a joke about the habit, not about them.
- Wedding: "I'm like your chacha only" may land differently for people who have heard it in real life. Observe reactions.
- Mentions of "police" and "112" in the endings should feel reassuring, not like a threat. Ask participants how they read them.
- The family-search ending ("Mummy Has Entered the Chat") might feel too close to home for some players. Observe reactions.
